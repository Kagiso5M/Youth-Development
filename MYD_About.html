All about Mahikeng Youth Development

  1. Mission, Vision, and Values

    • Vission: To be a leading catalyst for youth empowerment in Mahikeng, creating a generation of skilled, innovative, and self-reliant young people who drive inclusive economic growth in the North West Province.
    • Our Mission: To combat youth unemployment and poverty by providing accessible education support, skills development, and entrepreneurial opportunities, specifically targeting the NEET (Not in Education, Employment, or Training) demographic in the Ngaka Modiri Molema District.
    • Our Values:
      • Ubuntu: Fostering community support and collective upliftment.
      • Empowerment: Equipping youth with practical tools for self-sufficiency.
      • Integrity: Maintaining transparency and accountability in all operations.
      • Innovation: Embracing creative solutions to address local socio-economic challenges.

  2. Our Story:

    • Founded: Established in response to the high rates of unemployment and school dropouts observed in Mahikeng and surrounding rural areas (such as the villages served by organizations like C.O.R.R.U.P.T Youth Development) .
    • Evolution: What began as a small-scale tutoring initiative has grown into a holistic development programme. We recognized that academic support alone is insufficient; thus, we expanded our scope to include career guidance, digital literacy, and entrepreneurship, aligning with provincial initiatives like the Youth Summit held in Mahikeng to connect learners with career opportunities .
    • Community Impact: We have successfully bridged the gap between education and employment by partnering with local schools and leveraging resources from the North-West University (NWU) and local government programs to provide real-world exposure to our youth.

  3. Credibility and Compliance

    • Legal Status:Registered as a Non-Profit Company (NPC) with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC).
    • Tax Compliance:Holds a valid Tax Clearance Certificate and is registered as a Public Benefit Organisation (PBO) with SARS, allowing us to issue Section 18A tax certificates to donors.
    • B-BBEE and Sectoral Alignment:We are fully compliant with B-BBEE codes, qualifying as a Level 1 or Level 2 contributor (depending on your specific certification). Our programmes are aligned with the National Youth Policy and the North West Provincial Growth and Development Strategy, focusing on sectors like agriculture, ICT, and manufacturing identified by the provincial government as key growth areas .

  4. Footprint and Scope

    • Geographical Reach:Our primary operations are based in the Mahikeng Local Municipality, with active outreach programmes extending into the broader Ngaka Modiri Molema District. We specifically target under-resourced areas and rural villages surrounding the town center, ensuring that youth from marginalized communities have access to our programmes.
